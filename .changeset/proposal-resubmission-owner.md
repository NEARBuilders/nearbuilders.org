Resubmitting an existing proposal is limited to the people it belongs to: the original submitter, the builder the proposal is about, an admin, or (for projects and events) someone who can edit that project or event, such as an accepted collaborator or org member. Core flows that set a resubmission policy (nominations, catalog claims) are unchanged.
